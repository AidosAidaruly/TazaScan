# TazaScan Privacy Policy

MVP draft. This document is not legal advice and should be reviewed before a large-scale public launch.

TazaScan helps users scan or search food products in Kazakhstan and view informational nutrition analysis.

## Data We Process

- Barcode and product search requests.
- Package photos uploaded with product submissions.
- Optional product submission fields such as barcode, product name, brand, category, ingredients, and nutrition facts.
- Local scan history and favorites stored on the user's device.
- Server logs needed for security, debugging, abuse prevention, and reliability.

## Data We Do Not Collect In This MVP

TazaScan does not require registration and does not ask for user name, email, phone number, or social login.

## Local History And Favorites

History and favorites are stored locally on the device. They are not synced to the backend in this MVP.

## Product Submissions

Users may submit product data and package photos for moderation. Uploaded images may be retained to review, verify, or improve product database quality.

## Admin Moderation

Administrators can review submissions, approve products, reject submissions, or request correction. User-submitted data is treated as unverified until moderated.

## No Sale Of Personal Data

TazaScan does not sell personal data.

## Deletion Requests

For MVP deletion requests, contact the project owner with enough information to identify the uploaded submission, such as barcode, product name, approximate submission time, or image details.

## Health Disclaimer

Nutrition scores are informational only and are not medical advice.
