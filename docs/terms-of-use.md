# TazaScan Terms Of Use

MVP draft. This document is not legal advice and should be reviewed before a large-scale public launch.

## Informational Use

TazaScan provides product data, nutrition facts, and scoring for informational purposes. Product data may contain errors, be incomplete, or become outdated.

Users should verify product packaging before making purchase or dietary decisions.

## No Medical Advice

TazaScan does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or personalized medical advice. Consult a qualified professional for health decisions.

## User Submissions

Users may submit product information and package photos for moderation. Do not upload abusive, illegal, misleading, executable, or unrelated content.

## Product Data Errors

If you believe product data, images, ingredients, or scoring are wrong, report the issue through the project owner or moderation process.

## Intellectual Property

Uploaded images should be used only to identify and verify products. Rights holders can request review or removal of content through the project owner.

## Service Changes

TazaScan may change features, data, or scoring logic as the product evolves.
